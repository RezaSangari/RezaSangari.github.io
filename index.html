Open-source software

MFO–BOA–PDE–Solver

Hybrid Moth-Flame Optimization (MFO) + Butterfly Optimization Algorithm (BOA) with B-spline collocation for nonlinear PDEs. M.Sc. thesis implementation, Damghan University (2026).

Thesis benchmarks (30 runs, mean RMSE): heat equation 3.2×10−5; Burgers’ equation 2.8×10−4 — about 62% lower than MFO and 69% lower than BOA; diffusion equation 3.9×10−4 (M.Sc. thesis; baselines in the repository README).

Companion paper: MFO–BOA article, Submitted Oct 2026 (see Publications).

GitHub repository Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24222698 Version 1.1.5
